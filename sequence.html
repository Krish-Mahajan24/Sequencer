Build a sequence

Sequencer treats every track as a node and the tempo/energy/mood gap between any two tracks as an edge weight. Pick a starting tempo — it walks the nearest unplayed node each step (nearest-neighbor), then runs a 2-opt pass that swaps segments of the route to iron out any remaining big jumps.
